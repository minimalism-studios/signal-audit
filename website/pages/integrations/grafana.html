Beyond alert delivery

Grafana detects the change. Signal Audit explains what it means.

Grafana is excellent at identifying when production behavior moves outside an expected range. But an alert alone does not explain significance, urgency, likely impact, or what the team should do next.

Signal Audit adds an operational-intelligence layer between detection and response.

Integration capabilities

Built for the full Grafana alert lifecycle.

01

Authenticated ingestion

Grafana sends production events through a dedicated, authenticated webhook endpoint.

02

Alert normalization

Grafana payloads are converted into a consistent internal signal format for analysis and tracking.

03

AI interpretation

Signal Audit evaluates operational risk, urgency, likely impact, and recommended response.

04

Recovery correlation

Resolved alerts update the original signal lifecycle instead of generating disconnected records.

05

Slack delivery

Alerts and AI analysis are delivered as parent messages and threaded responses.

06

Persistent history

Every received, analyzed, delivered, failed, and resolved state remains part of the operational record.
