One intelligence layer

The observability platform can change. The Signal Audit workflow remains consistent.

Every integration follows the same operational pipeline: receive the production signal, normalize vendor-specific context, analyze its significance, preserve its lifecycle, and deliver guidance where the team already works.

That makes Signal Audit an extensible intelligence layer across your production stack—not a one-off connection to a single monitoring tool.
