Beyond monitor notification

Datadog detects production change. Signal Audit explains what it means.

Datadog monitors identify when system behavior crosses an expected threshold. The alert provides evidence that something changed, but teams still need to determine urgency, operational impact, likely causes, and the next place to investigate.

Signal Audit adds an operational-intelligence layer between Datadog detection and engineering response.
