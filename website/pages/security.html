Security & Architecture

Operational intelligence without becoming another control plane.

Signal Audit is designed to sit downstream of your production systems. It receives operational telemetry through controlled integrations, interprets what those signals mean, and delivers structured intelligence into the workflows your team already uses.

Operating Principles

Clear boundaries by design.

Signal Audit is designed as an interpretation layer between operational telemetry and the workflows where engineering teams make decisions.

01

Authenticated ingestion

Supported telemetry sources send signals through authenticated integration endpoints rather than an unrestricted public intake.

02

No infrastructure control plane

Signal Audit interprets operational telemetry. It does not need to become the system responsible for controlling your production infrastructure.

03

Existing systems remain authoritative

Grafana, Datadog, OpenTelemetry sources, production applications, and other originating systems remain authoritative for the data and events they produce.

04

Workflow delivery

Signal Audit delivers interpretation into supported destinations such as Slack instead of requiring teams to move operational response into another monitoring console.

System Boundaries

What Signal Audit does—and what it does not need to do.

The distinction matters because Signal Audit is designed to interpret operational information without replacing the systems responsible for running or monitoring your environment.

Signal Audit does

  • Receive supported operational telemetry.
  • Normalize incoming signal data.
  • Interpret operational significance with AI.
  • Preserve signal lifecycle context.
  • Generate structured operational guidance.
  • Deliver intelligence into supported workflows.

Signal Audit does not need to

  • Replace your observability platform.
  • Become your production control plane.
  • Own the authoritative production state.
  • Replace your incident-response workflow.
  • Take control of the systems generating telemetry.
  • Become another monitoring destination.

Deployment Approach

Validate the integration before exposing production data.

Signal Audit integrations can be validated in staging or sandbox environments before production use. When appropriate, test data can be masked or obfuscated so integration behavior can be verified while keeping production environments isolated during implementation.

Shared Responsibility

Customers remain in control of what they send.

Signal Audit processes telemetry supplied through configured integrations. Customers remain responsible for determining which operational data is appropriate and authorized for transmission from their environment.

Customer
Controls the source environment and determines which telemetry is supplied to Signal Audit.
Signal Audit
Receives, interprets, preserves, and delivers supported operational signals.
