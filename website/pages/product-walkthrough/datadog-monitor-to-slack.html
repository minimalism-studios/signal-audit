Overview

Detection creates awareness. Interpretation creates understanding.

Datadog monitors notify teams when service behavior crosses a defined threshold. They can identify elevated error rates, latency, failed requests, dependency problems, or resource pressure.

Product Workflow 002 demonstrates how Signal Audit extends that workflow. A Datadog monitor event enters Signal Audit automatically, becomes a normalized production signal, is evaluated for operational meaning, recorded through its lifecycle, and delivered into Slack with clearer guidance for the responding team.

The Observability Gap

A monitor alert does not answer the operational question.

Datadog can show that error rates crossed a threshold. It can surface affected services, tags, hosts, and surrounding telemetry.

The interpretation burden still falls on engineers. Someone must decide whether the alert is isolated noise, a dependency failure, a developing degradation pattern, or an incident requiring immediate action.

Datadog identifies the production condition. Signal Audit explains its operational significance.

The Automated Workflow

A repeatable pipeline from detection to decision.

01

Receive

Datadog sends the monitor event through a secure, connection-specific webhook.

02

Validate

Signal Audit verifies authentication, connection identity, payload structure, and integration type.

03

Normalize

Datadog-specific fields become a consistent internal signal model.

04

Analyze

AI evaluates urgency, likely impact, operational context, and the recommended next action.

05

Deliver

The alert and its interpretation are posted into Slack as a parent message and threaded analysis.

06

Recover

A Datadog recovery event updates the original signal lifecycle.

Signal Audit Analysis

The alert becomes an operational interpretation.

Signal Audit analyzes the event using Datadog-specific context while producing a consistent decision format for the responding team.
