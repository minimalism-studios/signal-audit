FAQ

Questions before you buy?

What do I need to provide?

Existing alerts, telemetry, dashboards, operational context, integration access, or other materials relevant to the engagement you select.

Is Signal Audit a monitoring tool?

No. Signal Audit works with the production signals your organization already generates and transforms them into operational intelligence: what matters, why it matters, and what to do next.

Do professional services include the ongoing Signal Audit subscription?

Basic Implementation, the Slack Pilot, Operational Assessment, and the Guided 30-Day Engagement are professional-service engagements. Ongoing Signal Audit production use is available for $1,000 per month or $10,000 per year.

Is Signal Audit included with Managed Operational Intelligence?

Yes. Managed Operational Intelligence includes Signal Audit along with Minimalism's ongoing operational assessment, engineering guidance, analysis, recommendations, and executive reporting.

What happens after a pilot or guided engagement?

Your team can continue operating Signal Audit directly through a monthly or annual subscription, or move into Managed Operational Intelligence if you want Minimalism to remain continuously involved.
