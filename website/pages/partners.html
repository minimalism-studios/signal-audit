SIGNAL AUDIT / PARTNER & REFERRAL BRIEF

Better signals.
Clearer context.
Stronger decisions.

Signal Audit transforms monitoring alerts into structured, actionable intelligence, helping engineering teams understand production issues faster while preserving human oversight.

PARTNER OPPORTUNITY GUIDE Explore the brief ↓
01 / 06 IDEAL CUSTOMER

Who we're looking to work with

Growing B2B SaaS and technology companies, typically 20–500 employees, operating production infrastructure with Datadog or Grafana.

The strongest fit is a company already investing in monitoring infrastructure that needs more intelligence from the signals it collects.

02 / 06 DECISION-MAKERS

Who makes the decision

CTOs, CIOs, VPs of Engineering, Heads of Infrastructure, SRE leaders, and DevOps leaders.

03 / 06 OPERATIONAL CHALLENGES

The problems we solve

Alert overload. Too many alerts without enough context.

Interpretation burden. Engineers spending excessive time interpreting monitoring data.

Communication gaps. Difficulty communicating operational issues to stakeholders.

Trust and oversight. Need for trustworthy AI-assisted operational interpretation.

04 / 06 THE SOLUTION

What Signal Audit delivers

Structured interpretation of existing monitoring signals, persistent Signal History, and delivery into Slack.

AI-assisted operational intelligence within stakeholder-defined boundaries and human oversight.

05 / 06 COMMERCIAL OFFERING

Simple platform pricing.

Monthly subscription. $1,000 per month.

Annual subscription. $10,000 per year.

Implementation and pilot services are available separately.

A good introduction sounds like:

“I know a CTO whose engineering team uses Datadog, receives a lot of alerts, and needs a better way to understand and act on them.”
