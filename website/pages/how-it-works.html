How Signal Audit Works

From production signal to operational intelligence.

Signal Audit connects to the observability platforms that already understand your production environment, analyzes incoming signals, preserves their history, and delivers decision-ready guidance directly into your team's workflow.

01 Production environment

Your systems generate operational signals.

Services, infrastructure, dependencies, deployments, and user activity continuously produce evidence about system behavior.
