Frequently Asked Questions

What is Signal Audit?

Signal Audit helps engineering teams separate signal from noise, identify emerging patterns, and understand operational risk from the telemetry they already collect.

Is Signal Audit an observability platform?

No. Signal Audit works alongside your existing observability tools. It focuses on interpretation rather than data collection.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

No. Signal Audit is designed to work with the systems and workflows your team already uses.

What kinds of signals can be analyzed?

Application telemetry, infrastructure metrics, alerts, incidents, logs, performance trends, and operational events.

What do we receive from a Signal Audit?

A structured assessment that includes findings, signal classifications, risk indicators, observability gaps, and recommended actions.

How long does a Signal Audit take?

Most audits are delivered within five business days.

How does the Slack integration work?

Signal Audit can deliver findings and recommendations directly within Slack to make operational intelligence accessible to engineering teams.

Who is Signal Audit for?

Engineering leaders, SRE teams, DevOps teams, platform teams, and organizations looking to improve operational visibility and decision-making.

How is pricing structured?

Signal Audit is available as a one-time assessment or as a pilot engagement that includes Slack deployment and ongoing usage.

Will Signal Audit replace my engineers?

No. Signal Audit is designed to help teams make sense of operational signals faster and focus attention on what matters most.
